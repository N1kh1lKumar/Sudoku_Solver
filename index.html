Backtracking playground

Sudoku Solver

Enter a puzzle, validate it, and solve it in your browser.

Your puzzle

Use numbers from 1 to 9. Leave empty cells blank or enter 0.

How it works

1. The board is checked for invalid values and duplicate numbers.

2. A valid board is solved with a backtracking algorithm.

3. If no solution exists, the original entries stay unchanged.
